01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Description

Creating a space where OSCE exams are held, alongside an area for other students to have their lectures, is a challenging task. However, in this project, we achieved both: providing medical students with a dedicated area for their exams while also engaging other students within the same building. The OSCE stage of a medical student's life is often the most stressful, yet the clearest and calmest. To reflect this, we used a clear monolithic style in the OSCE area, employing concrete material to express that stage. On the other hand, the lives of other students are more adventurous, unstable, and chaotic. To mirror this, we used a tectonic style and a high-tech approach to design their spaces using glass and steel.

Keywords: OPEN / CLOSED — CALM / LOUD — CLEAR / PATTERNED — TECTONIC / MONOLITHIC
