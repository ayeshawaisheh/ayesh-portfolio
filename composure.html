Composure - Center of Excellence exterior and interior dual rhythms
01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project Concept & Statement

A center that gives medical students a dedicated environment for OSCE exams while sharing the building with other students' lectures — two very different rhythms of use held within one structure. Explored through the pairings open/closed, calm/loud, clear/patterned, and tectonic/monolithic.

01. Spatial Duality

Harmonizes high-stress exam environments with open, relaxed lecture and social commons.

02. Acoustic Zoning

Utilizes monolithic concrete masses to filter sound between loud gathering halls and quiet examination rooms.

03. Tectonic Light

Employs patterned facade louvers that control direct sunlight while providing natural ventilation.
