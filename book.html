About

A way of designing,
not just a set of drawings.

I approach architecture through research first — reading site, community, and material before reaching for form. From studio work to international workshops, that habit shapes everything I design.

Every project starts with the site's own conditions — climate, material, and the people who will use the space — before any form takes shape. This research-led habit extends from a professional internship to teaching juniors.

Competitions, charrettes, and workshops across Jordan, Italy, and beyond — always searching for design that serves the people around it.
