01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Description

A 3,000 m² library in Amman, Jordan, developed through a study of Richard Meier's architectural principles. His influence is reflected throughout the project in the plans, elevations, details, proportions, and spatial layering. The design explores geometric order, proportion, light, and the relationship between solid and void, maintaining a consistent architectural language from the overall composition to the smallest details.
