University of Jordan — Integrated Artery Framework

Project Type Campus Masterplan / Public Space
Timeline / Year 2025
Client / Status Academic / University Project
01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Description

Addresses the University of Jordan's Engineering and Science sector's critical spatial identity crisis—characterized by fragmented public spaces, visually weak entrance gates, and aesthetic dissonance—by implementing a strategic "Integrated Artery Framework" that treats campus circulation as a high-visibility living pulse. This transformative intervention unifies the campus through a continuous red arterial path that directs movement from redefined, functional gateways to a revitalized Science Court, successfully transforming dormant transition zones into high-energy social hubs.

03. DESIGN APPLICATION — VILLAGE

Science Court

The redesign of the Science Court and the 'Village' revitalizes underutilized zones—specifically the open area fronting the Biology Department—into a high-energy social hub. By reconfiguring the landscape and introducing modular metal kiosks alongside a flexible seating system, the space is transformed into an active destination for students. This intervention is unified by the surgical red-painting of the Geology Building's perforated brick facade, harmonizing tactile heritage with modern functionality and the crimson arterial path to create a vibrant, integrated academic environment.

05. THE RED LINK

The Red Link

Linking the Engineering and Science gates to the campus core, a Red Arterial Path serves as a bold wayfinding prototype guided by red elements and signage. To resolve architectural dissonance, the IT Faculty is wrapped in a red perforated metal skin, harmonizing its distant form with the project's new identity and creating a unified, revitalized campus narrative.

06. THE RED ENTRANCES

The Red Entrances

The campus thresholds are redefined through the 'Red Gate' at the Engineering entrance, featuring a modern design that aligns with the new court's identity. At the Science Gate, a new multi-story parking structure optimizes space and alleviates vehicular pressure within the campus core. Both entrances are seamlessly integrated into the Red Arterial Link, ensuring a unified and efficient transition from the university gates to its vibrant heart.

07. THE RED IDENTITY

The Red Identity

To preserve the spatial identity and visual coherence of the intervention, all furniture elements were designed within a unified language — consistent in material, color, and form. From seating to lighting and signage, every piece reinforces the red arterial narrative, creating a seamless and recognizable campus vocabulary that ties the entire project together.
