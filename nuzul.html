Status Architectural Concept
01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Description

This ecolodge draws from the vernacular spirit of Iraq al-Amir, where architecture is rooted in place and community. The design embraces in-between spaces and thresholds as moments to slow the pace, inviting pause, reflection, and sensory awareness. Through tactile tectonics and a human-scaled layout, the lodge offers a grounded experience — one that feels timeless, connected, and deeply woven into its natural and cultural setting.
