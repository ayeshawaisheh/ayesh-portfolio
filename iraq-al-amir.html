01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project Concept & Statement

An interdisciplinary charrette proposing community-centered interventions for heritage preservation, eco-tourism, and local handicraft empowerment in Iraq al-Amir.

01. Heritage Sensitive

Respects historic archaeological sites while creating modern visitor facilities.

02. Economic Empowerment

Incorporates spaces for the local Women's Cooperative for craft production and sales.

03. Eco-Trail Network

Designs low-impact pedestrian pathways linking caves, springs, and village heritage sites.
