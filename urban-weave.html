PROJECT DETAIL // URBAN MASTERPLAN / TRANSPORT HUB

The Urban Weave

Al-Salt Gateway — Transport & Public Hub
Location / Site As-Salt, Jordan
Project Type Urban / Transport Hub
Timeline / Year 2024 - Present
Client / Status BRT Integration Proposal
01. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Description

The Urban Weave reconfigures Al-Salt's primary urban gateway, currently a chaotic and fragmented transport node lacking identity, comfort, and safety, into a structured, human-centered public hub. The site suffers from spatial conflict between buses, pedestrians, informal commerce, and surrounding urban activities, creating an inefficient and disordered environment. The proposal introduces a layered system that organizes transportation, vending activities, pedestrian movement, and public social spaces into a coherent urban structure. Rather than treating transportation as an isolated infrastructure problem, the project transforms the gateway into an active civic space that reflects Al-Salt's urban identity and supports everyday social interaction. Through strategic zoning, improved circulation, shaded public spaces, and integrated commercial activity, the project aims to create a more legible, accessible, and inclusive urban environment. The proposal also anticipates the city's future mobility developments, including the BRT system, positioning the gateway as a flexible urban connector between transportation, commerce, and public life.

The project is located at Al-Salt's primary urban gateway, where the city's northern entrance connects it to Amman and surrounding areas. The site sits within a valley environment near Ain Al-Jadour, where movement routes, public transportation, commercial activities, and everyday urban life converge without a clear spatial structure. The proposal rethinks the gateway not simply as a transportation node, but as an important urban and civic space that organizes existing movement while creating a stronger connection between transportation, commerce, public life, and the identity of Al-Salt.

The project does not simply redesign a transportation station; it reimagines the gateway as a living piece of the city. Its uniqueness lies in weaving transportation, commerce, heritage, and social interaction into one integrated urban system. By responding to the existing informal activities rather than erasing them, the project transforms existing urban patterns into an organized and inclusive public realm. It creates a flexible gateway capable of evolving with Al-Salt's future mobility needs while maintaining a strong connection to the city's identity.

05. PROJECT VISUALS

Design Expression & Materiality

Plaza view — facade composition and architectural details; parametric perforated sheets on the southeast elevation reduce heat exposure to the building mass, applying green architecture principles

The project blends Al-Salt's architectural heritage with a contemporary design language. The ground floor is rooted in traditional Salti expression — stone vaults, local materiality, and pedestrian-scale arcades that reference the city's historic fabric. In contrast, the upper floors (first and second levels) adopt a bolder, more futuristic language with high-tech detailing, lighter structural elements, and a distinct visual identity. This vertical dialogue between tradition and innovation reflects the project's ambition to bridge Salt's past with its future, creating a gateway that is both grounded in place and forward-looking in its urban vision.
