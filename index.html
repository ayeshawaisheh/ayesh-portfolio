PROJECT 01

The Urban Weave

Al-Salt Gateway — Transport & Public Hub

Reconfigures As-Salt's primary urban gateway — a chaotic, fragmented transport node — into a structured, human-centered hub. A layered system organizes buses, vending activity, and public social space, supporting the city's growth and upcoming BRT development.

LocationAs-Salt, Jordan
TypeUrban / Transport Hub

PROJECT 02

Composure

Center of Excellence — OSCE Exam & Lecture Facility

A center that gives medical students a dedicated environment for OSCE exams while sharing the building with other students' lectures — two very different rhythms of use held within one structure. Explored through the pairings open/closed, calm/loud, clear/patterned, and tectonic/monolithic.

TypeAcademic / Medical Facility
ConceptComposure

PROJECT 03

The Red Mark

University of Jordan — Integrated Artery Framework

A strategic "Integrated Artery Framework" that treats campus circulation as a high-visibility living pulse — unifying the campus through a continuous red arterial path from redefined gateways to a revitalized Science Court.

TypeCampus Masterplan / Public Space

PROJECT 04

Back to Modernity

Library Project — Amman, Jordan | 3,000 m²

A 3,000 m² library exploring Richard Meier's principles — geometric order, proportion, light, and the relationship between solid and void, from overall composition to the smallest detail.
