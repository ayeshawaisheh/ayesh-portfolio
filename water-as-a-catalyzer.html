01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project Concept & Statement

An urban and architectural proposal using water as a catalyst for rebuilding Gaza's sea port, developed with a Mediterranean-wide team. Recognized with an Honorable Mention in the UMAR Competition.

01. Maritime Regeneration

Re-activates port infrastructure through sustainable water management and eco-restoration.

02. Community Anchor

Designs public maritime plazas that reconnect civic life with the Mediterranean seafront.

03. Modular Construction

Proposes phased, resilient structures using locally sourced and recycled materials.
