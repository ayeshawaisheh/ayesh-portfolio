01 · ARCHITECTURAL PRACTICE

Panorama Consulting Engineers — Intern Architect

The desk at Panorama Consulting Engineers faced site visits and deadlines. Over two summer months I carried residential and commercial work — most vividly the Hawamdeh project, developed independently from construction documents into a design the senior architect approved without a single revision. Coordination with civil, electrical, and mechanical teams turned "drawing" from a verb into a negotiation, and the general manager's door stayed open just long enough to learn how much of the job is people.

02 · INTERNATIONAL WORKSHOP

UCAN — Liberty Square, Brescia, Italy

Seven days in Brescia, six countries around the table, one site in Ukraine. The UCAN International Workshop asked us to rebuild Liberty Square as a public space while the square's namesake was being defended elsewhere. The proposal anchored the new square in local heritage — rapid site research, participatory design sessions, and a final submission drawn between late nights and early discussions. Cross-cultural exchange was not a slide in the brief; it was the method.

03 · COMPETITION SUMMER SCHOOL

UMAR — "Water as a Catalyzer", Honorable Mention

Two weeks, a Mediterranean-wide table, and a brief to revive Gaza's sea port through water as a catalyst. Hosted between the German Jordanian University and the University of Birzeit, the UMAR Summer School pushed the project to an Honorable Mention — a research-heavy, water-driven framework weaving infrastructure, ecology, and public life back into a conflict-affected coast. 04 · LEADERSHIP & UNION LIFE

Chapters, elections, and a modernization program.

The serious side of student life. Between 2021 and 2026 the union was a second faculty: election nights, accreditation letters, and a two-year leadership program funded by the Dutch Embassy. It is where I learned that architecture shares its real subject with politics — space for people, and the patience to convince them.

AIAS — University of Jordan Chapter, PR & HR Team Head

As Public Relations & Human Resources Team Head at the AIAS — University of Jordan Chapter, I help run the engine behind the chapter's calendar — recruitment, onboarding, and internal communication that keep a student-led organization actually moving. The pay-off is the events themselves: organizing hands-on workshops where students touch tools and materials before they touch deadlines; coordinating project critiques that turn desk reviews into real conversations; and bringing practitioners into the room — speakers pulled from the job market who show what the drawing board becomes in Amman's real offices. And when the city itself invited us out, we joined Greater Amman Municipality's downtown workshops — swapping the classroom for the old city's streets, and carrying an architecture student's instinct into the public realm.

Part 01 — Student Representative, Faculty of Engineering · 2024

Elected as one of the eight official representatives of the Faculty of Engineering in the 2024 Student Union elections, the mandate began where campus life actually hurts — scheduling conflicts, registration barriers, and spaces that had stopped working for students. What followed was a slow, quiet service record: resolving academic and administrative issues, bringing services back to students, and organizing both curricular and non-curricular activities — from a departmental redesign that the JEA funded and executed, to reopening the Faculty's Reading Hall after more than a year with a JO Academy grant, from coordinating the Employability Readiness Program's 300+ participants to helping organize the launch ceremony of the Aircraft Maintenance Engineering program with Joramco. Part 02 — Iimar & Tatweer · Student Campaign Director, 2026

Four years after taking a seat, I ran on the other side of the table — leading the campaigns themselves. As campaign director I carried Tatweer's incumbency while building Iimar from nothing: an architecture-only slate that, against multi-department coalitions, won a dedicated Faculty seat (3 of 8 seats, 660 votes) — the first single-department list to reach Faculty-level representation at the University of Jordan.
