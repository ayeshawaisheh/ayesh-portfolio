01. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Project Concept & Statement

A context-sensitive proposal for Liberty Square, Ukraine, developed during a seven-day intensive international workshop hosted by the University of Brescia.

01. Civic Identity

Establishes a flexible urban square for community gathering, remembrance, and democratic life.

02. Adaptive Landscape

Incorporates green infrastructure, rain gardens, and permeable paving for urban resilience.

03. Rapid Prototyping

Developed through an intensive 7-day international collaborative charrette.
